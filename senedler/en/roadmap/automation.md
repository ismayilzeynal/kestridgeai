# Automation - Project Roadmap

Every step from first contact to handover. The defining rule: the old
process is **never switched off until the new automation is proven**.

| # | Stage | Duration | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Inquiry & response | 1 to 2 business days | Meeting time scheduled |
| 1 | Discovery call | 30 to 45 min | Which processes, what gain |
| 2 | Process mapping | 3 to 7 business days | As-is process approved in writing |
| 3 | Proposal & agreement | 2 to 3 business days | To-be design + price signed |
| 4 | Kickoff | ~1 week | Test accounts, environment ready |
| 5 | Build | in sprints | Workflows running in test |
| 6 | Parallel run | min. 2 weeks | Results match the old process |
| 7 | Cutover | 1 to 2 days | Process runs automatically |
| 8 | Stabilization | 2 to 4 weeks | Stable under real load |
| 9 | Handover | 2 to 3 days | Runbook, training, access revoked |

## The steps

**0. Inquiry & response.** We reply within 1 to 2 business days and schedule
the first meeting.

**1. Discovery call.** We listen for where time is being lost and where
errors appear. The 1 to 3 processes with the highest payoff are selected.

**2. Process mapping.** We document the process step by step together
with the people who run it today (screen recordings welcome). **The as-is
process is approved by the client in writing** - automation is only built
on top of a correctly understood process.

**3. Proposal & agreement.** The to-be (automated) process design,
behavior for exceptions, scope, timeline and price. Acceptance criteria:
what share of the process runs automatically, and what happens on error.

**4. Kickoff.** Test accounts are obtained for every system to be
integrated, the test environment is set up, the communication channel is
agreed. Rules: see "Delivery Rules - Automation".

**5. Build.** Workflows are built: via API where one exists, RPA only
where there is none. Error handling + notifications at every step. Weekly
status + a working demo.

**6. Parallel run.** The new automation runs **side by side with the old
process for at least 2 weeks**; results are compared daily. If they
diverge, the cause is found and fixed and the period is extended. The old
process is untouched during this stage.

**7. Cutover.** Once results match, the old process is retired on an
agreed day. The path back to the old process stays open.

**8. Stabilization.** Monitoring under real load, immediate fixes,
tuning where needed. **We do not hand over and leave until the process
provably works.**

**9. Handover.** Runbook (how to stop, restart, and resolve typical
errors), team training, access revocation. After that - the agreed
support arrangement.
