# Analytics - Project Roadmap

Every step from first contact to handover. The defining rule: no
dashboard is opened to users **until its numbers are reconciled against
the source systems**.

| # | Stage | Duration | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Inquiry & response | 1 to 2 business days | Meeting time scheduled |
| 1 | Discovery call | 30 to 45 min | Which decisions need what data |
| 2 | Data source audit | 3 to 5 business days | Source list and their condition |
| 3 | Proposal & agreement | 2 to 3 business days | KPI list + scope signed |
| 4 | Kickoff | ~1 week | Read-only access, environment ready |
| 5 | Data pipeline | 1 to 3 weeks | Data collected and cleaned automatically |
| 6 | Dashboard build | in sprints | Weekly reviews, iterative refinement |
| 7 | Validation (sign-off) | 3 to 5 business days | Numbers reconciled, approval given |
| 8 | Go-live + training | 2 to 3 days | The team can use it on their own |
| 9 | Stabilization | 2 to 4 weeks | Refreshes stable, alerts working |
| 10 | Handover | 1 to 2 days | Docs, access revoked |

## The steps

**0. Inquiry & response.** We reply within 1 to 2 business days and schedule
the first meeting.

**1. Discovery call.** What decisions do they want to make by looking at
the numbers? We start from that question - a dashboard is a decision
tool, not decoration. A first KPI list emerges.

**2. Data source audit.** Where the data lives (CRM, ERP, databases,
spreadsheets), its quality, and whether it is sufficient to compute the
KPIs. If data is missing, we say so openly and plan around it.

**3. Proposal & agreement.** Precise KPI definitions (**metric
definitions - how each metric is computed, in writing**), the dashboard
list, refresh frequency, timeline and price. Definitions are approved up
front so there is no "why is this number like that" dispute at the end.

**4. Kickoff.** **Read-only** access to the sources is obtained, the
working environment is set up, the status day is agreed.
Rules: see "Delivery Rules - Analytics".

**5. Data pipeline.** Data is pulled automatically from the sources,
cleaned, and consolidated in one place (warehouse). Alerts are set up for
pipeline failures.

**6. Dashboard build.** Iterative: finished parts are shown every week,
feedback collected, adjustments made. Users are involved from the start.

**7. Validation.** Every dashboard figure is reconciled with the source
system. The client gives a **written "the numbers are correct" sign-off** - only then do we go live.

**8. Go-live + training.** Users get access, a training session is held
(recorded), and a short user guide is provided.

**9. Stabilization.** Refresh stability, data freshness and alerts are
monitored; any issue is fixed immediately. **We do not hand over and
leave until the system provably works.**

**10. Handover.** Pipeline documentation, metric definitions, runbook,
access revocation. After that - the agreed support arrangement.
