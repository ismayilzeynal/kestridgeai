# IT Security - Project Roadmap

Every step from first contact to handover. The iron rule: **no testing
starts without written authorization** - not even a basic scan.

| # | Stage | Duration | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Inquiry & response | 1 to 2 business days | Meeting time scheduled |
| 1 | Discovery call | 30 to 45 min | Need is clear (audit / pentest / monitoring) |
| 2 | Scope & written authorization | 2 to 4 business days | Signed authorization + scope list |
| 3 | Proposal & agreement | 2 to 3 business days | Methodology, timeline, price signed |
| 4 | Kickoff | 2 to 3 days | Test windows, emergency contacts agreed |
| 5 | Execution (assessment) | 1 to 3 weeks | Testing completed within scope |
| 6 | Report | 3 to 5 business days | Findings by severity + remediation steps |
| 7 | Remediation support | per plan | Vulnerabilities closed |
| 8 | Retest | 2 to 5 business days | Fixes verified |
| 9 | Final report & handover | 1 to 2 days | Clean status, access revoked |

## The steps

**0. Inquiry & response.** We reply within 1 to 2 business days and schedule
the first meeting.

**1. Discovery call.** What is needed: a security audit, a penetration
test, compliance preparation, or continuous monitoring? We get the
overall picture of the environment. The NDA is usually signed right here.

**2. Scope & written authorization.** An exact list of what will be
tested (IPs, domains, applications). **Written authorization from the
client's management** is obtained. Nothing outside the scope is touched.

**3. Proposal & agreement.** Methodology, test types, timeline, price,
report format. Tests that could cause a service outage enter the scope
only with separate, explicit consent.

**4. Kickoff.** Test windows (usually outside business hours), 24/7
emergency phone contacts on both sides, and a stop procedure are agreed.
Rules: see "Delivery Rules - IT Security".

**5. Execution.** Testing proceeds within the agreed scope and windows.
**A critical vulnerability is reported directly within 24 hours - we do
not wait for the report.**

**6. Report.** Findings ranked Critical / High / Medium / Low, each with:
description, evidence, business risk, and a concrete remediation step. A
non-technical executive summary on top. Delivered only over an encrypted
channel.

**7. Remediation support.** The client fixes, or we fix - per the
agreement. Either way, we keep answering questions throughout.

**8. Retest.** Closed vulnerabilities are re-verified. One retest is
included at no extra charge.

**9. Final report & handover.** Clean status is documented, all of our
access is revoked and the revocation is confirmed in the handover
document. **We do not close the engagement until we are certain the
vulnerabilities are actually closed.**

## Addendum for the continuous monitoring service

If monitoring is ordered, stages 5 to 9 become: connecting log sources →
2 to 4 weeks of tuning (clearing false alarms) → escalation matrix sign-off
→ switch to 24/7 operation → monthly reports.
